# Stewards

The place is defined by its stewards. A small group governs the agent together, through these notes. They set its voice (`place.md`), what it reads (`sources.yml`), who it can point to (`people.yml`), and what it has been corrected on (`corrections/`).

## How decisions get made

Change these to fit your place. A house may decide everything at a house meeting.

- Anything here changes by pull request. Stewards review. Nothing merges on its own.
- One steward can merge an event, a correction, or a proposal that looks right and harms no one. When in doubt, ask another steward.
- Changes to `place.md`, `STEWARDS.md`, `sources.yml`, or anything under `constituents/` wait for two stewards, or seven days with no objection, whichever comes first.
- Removing a source, a person, or a constituent is a steward decision and gets a line saying why.

## Reviewing a draft from the agent

Every draft carries what the person asked for in their words, what the agent changed, and why. Check:

1. Does the file match what they asked for? The agent showed them the exact text first, but read it anyway.
2. Is anything in it about a private individual? Events name hosts only if the host gave a name for that purpose. Corrections say how the person knows, not who told them.
3. For a correction: is the "how I know" good enough? A photo of the door, a call, being there. "I heard" is not enough; ask.
4. For a proposal: is there already a thing that does this? Point there and close, or merge and let people find each other.

Merge, or leave a comment. The agent never follows up on its own.

## Becoming a steward

Open a pull request that adds you to the table, with a line on why and which part of the place you are closest to. Current stewards talk it over there.

## Stepping back

Open a pull request removing your row. No explanation needed.
